# StitchTrack — Business Brief

## The Business

StitchTrack is an order and agreement management platform designed for small made-to-measure tailoring businesses in Nigeria.

Its target businesses handle garment orders, measurements, style references, prices, payments, fittings, and delivery commitments. These records may be spread across notebooks, WhatsApp, and existing digital systems.

Initial research with a fashion studio employee who has five years of experience confirmed the use of notebooks, WhatsApp, and a system for recordkeeping. The respondent identified organizing different delivery dates as their biggest difficulty.

StitchTrack aims to centralize garment records, make delivery commitments visible, and preserve changes to approved agreements.

## Target Users

**Primary users:** Independent tailors and small fashion studio operators, provisionally aged 23–55. The initial design assumes frequent smartphone use, varying technical experience, and occasionally unreliable internet access.

**Secondary users:** Clients ordering custom garments, provisionally aged 18–60. They need to review agreements, approve changes, check payments, and follow garment progress.

A client can use one StitchTrack account to view orders across multiple participating tailoring businesses. Each business's records remain isolated.

The age ranges, device usage, and connectivity assumptions require further validation.

## The One Thing That Must Work

StitchTrack must reliably show the current agreed commitment for each garment, including its delivery date, while preserving the history of approved changes.

A new agreement must never silently overwrite an earlier approved version.

## Three Recurring Business Questions

1. Which garments are due soon, overdue, or ready for delivery?
2. What style, price, measurements, and delivery date did we agree with this client, and what subsequently changed?
3. How much has the client paid, what remains outstanding, and what is preventing delivery?

The first question is supported by initial research. The others remain hypotheses to validate.

## Interface Direction

StitchTrack should feel organized, trustworthy, and approachable.

The dashboard should prioritize delivery dates, order statuses, pending approvals, and outstanding balances. Important information should be visible without navigating through several screens.

The interface should use clear language, readable typography, accessible status indicators, and responsive layouts suitable for smartphones and desktop computers.
