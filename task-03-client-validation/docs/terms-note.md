## Three different instruments

1. Platform Terms of Service would govern the operator-user service relationship.
2. Tailor-client garment agreements concern specific clothes items, measurements, pictures, prices and delivery commitments. Accepting platform terms cannot approve or alter those agreements.
3. A Privacy Policy explains actual processing and rights. Acknowledging that information is not blanket permission for every use of personal data. Any processing relying on consent needs its own justified, specific mechanism; a platform-terms checkbox cannot substitute for it.

The [field audit](field-justification.md) reserves a future signup-only, unchecked acceptance control. No checkbox, public policy page, consent workflow or acceptance record was added now.

## Owner and legal decisions still required

- Confirm the operating person/entity, address, monitored service/privacy contacts and intended contracting parties. No company registration is assumed.
- Confirm eligibility and treatment of minors before proposing an age rule.
- Determine controller/processor roles for account operations versus business-owned customer data; approve any data-processing agreement.
- Inventory actual hosting, database, email, image services, locations, recipients, transfers, cookies and logs. Libraries are not automatically processors; proposed vendors are not confirmed recipients.
- Select and document a lawful basis for each purpose; assess sensitive data, children, DPIA and any DPO/registration/audit obligations without assuming an exemption.
- Approve retention by data category, backups, dispute holds, deletion, export and rights handling. The PRD's proposed image schedule is not adopted as a legal promise.
- Confirm the pilot's commercial wording and any future charges, cancellation or refund position. The drafts add no refund scheme, fee schedule, liability cap, uptime guarantee or dispute forum.
- Review ownership/permissions for uploaded material, fair suspension and closure procedures, non-excludable rights, notices and changes to terms.
- Agree which terms version would be accepted, when fresh acceptance is needed and how future enforcement/recording will work.

## Review and release process

The owner first corrects factual gaps and decides the marked items. Counsel/privacy review then checks the complete current law and guidance, the real data map and the draft clauses. Implementation must be checked against the agreed wording so the policy does not promise unavailable features.

Only after explicit approval should a later change publish readable documents and link a registration checkbox. Authentication, consent enforcement and persistence require their own authorization. If legal review requires a PRD change, I will report the proposed correction and stop the affected implementation; I will not edit the PRD.

Until then, v0.1 has no effective date, no legal sign-off and no claim to bind a real account holder.
