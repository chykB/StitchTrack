## Three different instruments

1. Platform Terms of Service would govern the operator-user service relationship.
2. Tailor-client garment agreements concern specific clothes items, measurements, pictures, prices and delivery commitments. Accepting platform terms cannot approve or alter those agreements.
3. A Privacy Policy explains actual processing and rights. Acknowledging that information is not blanket permission for every use of personal data. Any processing relying on consent needs its own justified, specific mechanism; a platform-terms checkbox cannot substitute for it.

The [field audit](field-justification.md) reserves a future signup-only, unchecked acceptance control. No checkbox, public policy page, consent workflow or acceptance record was added now.

## Pre-launch checklist

- **Business name and identity:** Verify the StitchTrack name's availability and registration status with CAC and obtain advice on applicable requirements before operating under it. Personal operation does not establish name registration or limited liability. Supply appropriate publication details only after owner/legal confirmation.
- **NDPC major-importance assessment:** Review section 44 of the NDP Act and the operative GAID 2025, especially articles 8-9, against intended and actual processing. Record data-subject numbers, data types/sensitivity, service activities, scale and relevant designations; determine classification and any registration requirements, current procedures, timing and associated obligations. No threshold, exemption, registration or compliance status is assumed. Reassess if the processing changes. Personal operation or a free pilot must not be treated automatically as household/personal processing.
- **Roles and lawful bases:** Complete the activity-by-activity controller/processor and purpose/basis assessment, with any required instructions/agreements. A platform contract or checkbox alone does not establish a basis for every client's data.
- **Actual practices and release:** Confirm providers, hosting/email arrangements, locations/transfers, retention/deletion, contacts, rights handling, dispute provisions and other launch obligations. Complete qualified legal/privacy review and obtain explicit owner approval before public policies or acceptance controls are implemented. These checks remain pending; the list is not a compliance certificate.
