## A. Operator identity and authority

### Agreed direction

- StitchTrack will operate through a Nigerian company registered before public launch. This is a launch condition, not confirmation that registration has happened.
- A private company limited by shares is the preferred structure, pending incorporation advice and the owner's final registration choices.
- StitchTrack is the product brand; it need not be the company's legal name.
- The actual registered company will be the platform contracting party. Tailoring businesses will contract with that operator for platform services.
- Garment agreements remain between tailors and clients. Platform Terms acceptance cannot approve or amend a garment agreement.

### Facts and review still required

The actual company legal name, registration number/status, appropriate address, authority to contract and working service/privacy contact channels remain unresolved. The current website's responsible operator/controller must be identified separately rather than retroactively assumed to be an unverified future company.

Qualified Nigerian advice must confirm structure, brand/registration arrangements, contractual authority, eligibility/capacity and client platform-use conditions. The preferred form is not a final registration choice or a verified fact.

## B. Data and privacy arrangements

### Agreed direction

| Area | Intended arrangement and boundary |
| --- | --- |
| Platform accounts and administration | StitchTrack generally determines purposes for accounts, authentication, security and platform administration. Exact controller responsibilities require activity-specific legal review. |
| Business-owned records | Tailoring businesses generally determine purposes for customer and garment records. StitchTrack processes those records on their behalf where appropriate; one role label does not settle global client identity, linked-business access or dispute-evidence processing. |
| Business Data Processing Agreement | Prepare and review a separate business Data Processing Agreement before operational launch, covering applicable processor activities. It is distinct from Platform Terms and garment agreements; this record does not create or sign it. |
| Client privacy information | Support collection-point and invitation/portal notice delivery, with an accessible route for clients who have no email or portal access. Exact text, sender, timing and delivery mechanism remain to be confirmed. |
| Retention and deletion | Use record-category retention, controlled deletion and justified legal holds. Preserve necessary agreement history without promising indefinite image storage or treating archive status as deletion. |
| Rights requests | Establish a working privacy-request process with proportionate identity checks, routing between operator and business controllers, and access to applicable statutory rights. An account screen must not be the only route. |
| Incidents | Establish an incident-response procedure before processing real customer data, including responsibility, escalation and applicable notification handling. No procedure is represented as implemented by this record. |
| Providers and transfers | Record actual hosting, email, storage, backup and logging providers, roles, locations and remote-access arrangements before finalizing recipient and international-transfer disclosures. A software library is not evidence of a provider contract. |
| Advertising and AI | Do not introduce advertising tracking or AI processing of customer records into the initial pilot without a separate approved decision and PRD consistency review. This record authorizes neither. |

A business's platform contract does not establish a lawful basis for every use of a client's data. Platform Terms acceptance, receipt of privacy information and any purpose-specific consent remain separate.

### Provisional internal targets — not final policies

| Proposed figure | Scope | Qualification before adoption |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Three business days | Initial acknowledgement of a privacy request. | Not the final response deadline. Confirm the working calendar, monitored channel, routing and staffing; applicable legal response and incident-notification obligations take precedence. |
| 90 days | Proposed security-log retention window only. | Review necessity, actual logged fields, the retention trigger, deletion/backups and justified holds. This is not a retention period for images, agreements, payments or all other technical records, and no deletion job is authorized. |

The PRD's section 5 image lifecycle remains a separate proposed policy subject to privacy/legal review, including its notice, archiving, deletion and evidence-preservation requirements. The 90-day log proposal does not replace it.

Final activity-specific roles, lawful bases, business processing terms, sensitive-data/capacity safeguards, providers, transfer conditions, record-category retention and rights/incident arrangements require factual confirmation and qualified Nigerian privacy review. Applicable NDPC registration, DPO and impact-assessment obligations also require assessment; no compliance status is asserted.

## C. Operating procedures

### Agreed direction

- Provide working support and privacy contact channels before the related services are offered.
- Establish complaints/escalation, proportionate security-related suspension, review, account closure and appropriate export procedures. These may require operational arrangements as well as later authorized software work; this record does not implement them.
- Preserve legitimate record history during suspension and closure, subject to privacy rights, controlled retention and justified holds. Closure must not silently erase agreements or another business's records.
- Address platform misuse and record-integrity reports. StitchTrack does not automatically adjudicate garment-workmanship disputes or guarantee tailoring performance.
- Version public policies and provide appropriate notice and fresh acceptance for material Terms changes. Privacy notices and purpose-specific consent remain distinct; platform changes do not alter garment-agreement approvals.

### Provisional internal targets — not service guarantees

| Proposed figure | Scope | Qualification before adoption |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Two business days | Initial support response. | A planning target, not a resolution guarantee. Define business days, coverage, intake, staffing and escalation before adoption. |
| 30 days | Proposed export/request window on account closure. | Confirm its trigger, authorized requester, lawful export scope and practical availability. It is not an automatic deletion cutoff, permission for unrestricted export, or a limit on statutory privacy requests and other lawful access rights. |
| 30 days in advance, where practicable | Notice of material Terms changes. | A proposed notice target, not an effective date or contractual minimum already promised. Review legal/security exceptions, delivery, version records and fresh acceptance; do not use exceptions to defeat applicable rights. |

These five figures are provisional internal targets, not legal requirements, public promises, implemented features or final retention commitments. They cannot extend applicable legal periods or replace a lawful rights process.

Owner confirmation is still needed for contact details, business-day definitions, procedure owners, complaint/review routes, closure/export effects on pending orders and linked clients, notice/acceptance mechanisms, and how procedures will operate in practice.
