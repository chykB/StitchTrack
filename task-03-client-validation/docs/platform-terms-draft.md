# StitchTrack Platform Terms of Service - working draft

**v0.2 | 26 October 2026 | NOT APPROVED | NOT PUBLIC**

This is proposed wording for review, not an effective contract or evidence of legal advice. The intended service is described in the [PRD](../../docs/PRD.md). Current authentication remains UI-only. Clauses about future features are conditional on their implementation and approval.

**OWNER/LEGAL REVIEW markers must be resolved before publication.** No registered entity, effective date or contractual acceptance is asserted.

## 1. Who operates the service

The owner confirms that StitchTrack is a product intended to be operated personally by its founder, an individual in Nigeria, during the free initial pilot. No registered company or registered business name has been confirmed. The product name does not identify an incorporated entity.

**PRE-LAUNCH / OWNER/LEGAL REVIEW:** Check business-name availability, registration status and applicable CAC requirements before operating under the StitchTrack name. This draft does not establish a right to trade under that name. Confirm the individual's legal identity for the final contract, an appropriate address, monitored contact details, contracting parties and eligibility/capacity requirements. No inferred legal name, home address, registration number, contact details or age threshold is supplied.

## 2. Intended service and access

StitchTrack is intended to help independent tailors and small studios manage tailoring records. Where implemented, these may include orders with individual clothes items, measurement versions, fabric/style pictures, agreements, manual payment records and delivery updates.

Tailors use email/password accounts. Clients use an invitation and email-verification process without password registration. A tailor may access only their business's authorized records. A client may view only records linked through an authorized relationship; matching contact details alone does not grant access. These product rules do not mean authentication or access-control features are operational today.

**OWNER/LEGAL REVIEW:** Confirm the features and support actually available at release. Do not promise service levels, reminder delivery, perpetual storage or uninterrupted availability.

## 3. Platform terms are not garment agreements

These proposed terms address use of the platform. They do not set the price, design, fit or delivery terms for a garment and do not authorize StitchTrack or a tailor to approve an agreement for a client.

The intended product preserves submitted agreement versions and the approved measurement, fabric/style references, price and date. Changes affecting approved terms require the controlled approval workflow. Accepting platform terms cannot silently overwrite that evidence.

StitchTrack provides the record-management service, not the tailoring workmanship, and does not guarantee that workmanship. This distinction is not a blanket exclusion of the operator's own legal duties or users' statutory rights.

**OWNER/LEGAL REVIEW:** Confirm how tailoring disputes and evidence requests will be handled without claiming that StitchTrack adjudicates those disputes.

## 4. Money and commercial arrangements

The intended V1 records payments made directly to a tailor outside StitchTrack. A record is not independent proof of receipt. V1 does not process customer-to-tailor payments or provide full accounting.

The initial pilot is free, as confirmed by the owner and the PRD. This is not a promise of perpetual free service.

**OWNER/LEGAL REVIEW:** Confirm final pilot wording and any later commercial arrangements, cancellation or refund provisions before publication. No new refund arrangement, subscription, fee or payment guarantee is created by this draft.

## 5. Responsible use and uploaded information

Proposed rule for review: use only accounts and business/client records you are authorized to access. Do not knowingly submit unlawful material, compromise another person's access or misrepresent approval of garment terms.

The product needs permission to handle records and pictures for authorized service use; this draft does not assign ownership or grant an unlimited content licence.

**OWNER/LEGAL REVIEW:** Set the actual permissions, user notices, complaint/removal process and responsibilities for customer information. Confirm the allocation of platform and tailoring-business obligations.

## 6. Privacy and records

The separate [privacy draft](privacy-policy-draft.md) is a review document, not a published notice. Agreeing to a service contract is not blanket consent for every processing purpose.

The preliminary assessment is that the individual operator likely controls account-related processing. Customer records managed for tailoring businesses require a separate controller/processor assessment; no final allocation or processing agreement is established here.

**OWNER/LEGAL REVIEW:** Approve the purpose/lawful-basis assessment in the privacy draft, data map, providers, transfers, retention and rights process before writing definitive customer-facing privacy promises. Complete the NDPC major-importance registration assessment in the [pre-launch checklist](terms-note.md#pre-launch-checklist). Preservation of agreement history must be reconciled with applicable legal rights, not used to ignore them.

## 7. Fair service terms and statutory rights

Any final wording must be understandable and must not misrepresent unavailable features or use an unfair blanket waiver. The [FCCPA 2018](https://fccpc.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2022/07/FCCPA-2018.pdf), particularly sections 114 and 125-129, is a review reference; applicability and enforceability require counsel.

**OWNER/LEGAL REVIEW:** Draft any liability, remedies, suspension, termination, support and complaints clauses only after reviewing the actual service and non-excludable rights. No liability cap, mandatory arbitration, governing forum, refund rule or warranty exclusion is approved here.

## 8. Changes, closure and acceptance

**OWNER/LEGAL REVIEW:** Define fair notice of changes, any reacceptance process, closure/export handling, contact routes and the effective version. Product-history preservation does not authorize retrospective rewriting of a client's garment agreement.

A future signup flow may request affirmative acceptance of approved platform terms. This draft is not that request: no checkbox, acceptance enforcement or acceptance storage exists in the current interface.
