# StitchTrack Platform Terms of Service

**v1.0 candidate | Owner content review | NOT APPROVED | NOT PUBLIC**

No effective date has been assigned. This candidate is not an effective service contract. It describes the current interface and the planned operational V1 separately.

## 1. StitchTrack and the service covered

StitchTrack is being developed as a company-operated service in Nigeria for independent tailors, small fashion studios and their clients. The initial operational pilot is planned to be free. The product name is not confirmation of an incorporated company or registered legal entity.

The current release consists of an informational website and interface-only tailor signup, login and password-reset forms. It does not provide functioning customer accounts or the operational tailoring workflows described below.

## 2. Current forms and assessment acknowledgement

The forms check entered email and password values locally to provide validation feedback. They do not submit those values to an authentication service, create an account or session, or send password-reset emails. A valid local submission displays an unavailable-action message.

An explicitly enabled local development preview displays unapproved v0.2 legal drafts and an initially unchecked draft-review acknowledgement. Checking it demonstrates a form interaction only. It does not accept these candidate Terms, create a legal agreement or save an acceptance record.

The separate local database assessment tool is for synthetic enquiries. It is not account registration or a customer-order service. Do not enter real customer information or credentials into that tool.

## 3. Planned account access and business boundaries

Operational V1 is planned to provide email/password accounts for tailors and invitation-based, passwordless email verification for clients. Clients will not be asked to create passwords. A verified invitation authorizes each business relationship; matching email addresses or phone numbers alone will not grant access.

The planned service restricts each tailor to authorized business records and each client to records linked through a verified relationship. A client may have authorized links with more than one tailoring business. These are requirements for the operational service, not a claim that the current forms establish access.

Use only accounts and records you are authorized to access. Do not interfere with another person's access or misrepresent a client's approval.

## 4. Garment agreements and workmanship

Platform Terms govern use of StitchTrack. They do not set a garment's price, fit, design or delivery commitment and cannot approve a garment agreement on a client's behalf.

The planned V1 preserves submitted agreement versions for each clothes item, including the measurement version, fabric picture, style picture, price and delivery date. Only the authenticated, securely linked client can approve or reject their agreement. Changes to approved terms require a controlled proposal and client approval; rejected changes preserve the existing approved agreement.

StitchTrack does not perform tailoring work or guarantee a tailor's workmanship. Its planned role is to preserve and present the agreed records. It does not adjudicate tailoring disputes. This distinction does not exclude the service operator's own legal responsibilities or a user's statutory rights.

## 5. Free pilot and direct payments

The initial operational pilot is planned to be free. This does not promise that every future version will be free or authorize future charges. No subscription or payment-processing service is offered by the current interface.

Clients pay tailors directly outside StitchTrack. In operational V1, a tailor will record money received and any outstanding balance; StitchTrack will not collect, process or independently verify that payment. Payment entries will carry the notice:

> Payment recorded by tailor. Not independently verified by StitchTrack.

The planned records use approved prices and preserve corrections rather than silently overwriting payment history. An incomplete order total must not be presented as final. Refunds take place outside StitchTrack; the platform does not automatically refund money. These planned records do not independently establish that money was received.

## 6. Records, pictures and permitted use

Provide only information and material that you are entitled to supply for the service. Do not knowingly submit unlawful material or disclose someone else's private records without authority.

For operational V1, records and pictures are intended to be handled for authorized service purposes. Providing them does not transfer their ownership to StitchTrack or give it unrestricted rights to reuse them.

Each submitted garment agreement is intended to preserve one fabric picture and one style picture, with controlled changes and private or access-controlled storage. No image-upload or customer-image storage feature is currently available. Planned retention controls do not imply perpetual image availability.

## 7. Privacy, closure and records

The [Privacy Policy candidate](privacy-policy-v1.0-candidate.md) distinguishes current local form interaction, the isolated assessment experiment and planned V1 processing. Agreement to platform Terms is not blanket consent to all personal-data processing and does not approve a tailor-client garment agreement.

The current interface has no working account-closure, customer-record export or deletion feature. Preserving agreement evidence does not remove applicable privacy rights. Ending access or changing platform Terms does not authorize retrospective changes to a client's approved garment agreement.

## 8. Availability, changes and legal rights

Information about planned features is not a statement that they are currently available. The present interface does not provide operational delivery reminders, tailoring dashboards or guaranteed storage. No uptime, reminder-delivery or tailoring-performance guarantee is offered here.

A local draft acknowledgement is not acceptance of a later Terms version. A change to platform Terms does not silently amend a garment agreement. Nothing in these Terms excludes rights or remedies that cannot lawfully be excluded.
